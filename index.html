    About this trail

    Huntsville Space Trail maps the places where Huntsville's space history happened, from the Rocket Team's arrival in 1950 to the Artemis program today.

    How to read access labels

    • Open to the public: You can visit. Some sites charge admission.
    • Viewable from outside: You can see it from a street or parking lot, but you can't go in.
    • Restricted: Inside Redstone Arsenal or otherwise closed to visitors. Pins for restricted sites are approximate.

    The whole history

    Many members of von Braun's team built the V-2 missile in Nazi Germany, a program that used concentration-camp forced labor. This trail tells Huntsville's space story, including that part of it.
