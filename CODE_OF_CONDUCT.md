# Code of conduct

Huntsville Space Trail is a community history project. People join it from many backgrounds, including longtime residents, Rocket Team families, engineers, historians, and first-time visitors. Everyone deserves a respectful experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Disagree about facts by citing sources, not by attacking people.
- Welcome newcomers and people who don't write code.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information, including the home addresses of Rocket Team members or their families.
- Discuss difficult history, including the V-2 program's use of forced labor, honestly and with care.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or content of any kind
- Celebrating or excusing Nazi ideology or atrocities
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break this code of conduct, and can block people from the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers keep reports confidential.
